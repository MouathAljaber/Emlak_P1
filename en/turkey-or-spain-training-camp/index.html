Training camp in Turkey or Spain – the comparison

Both destinations are popular with clubs. Instead of a blanket winner, compare them on the criteria that really matter for your squad.

Request an offer

Both destinations are popular with clubs. Instead of a blanket winner, compare them on the criteria that really matter for your squad.

At a glance

  • Compare the same service package
  • Settle dates and weather first
  • Weight pitch quality and distances above room price
  • Ask about extras up front
01

Travel and flight time

From Germany, Austria and Switzerland flights to Antalya usually take around three and a half hours; Spain is a little shorter or longer depending on the region. Check flight schedules, equipment baggage rules and transfer times together.

02

Climate and training window

Antalya is mild in spring, autumn and winter and suits many preparation phases. Spain can also be mild depending on the coast. Your dates and expected weather decide.

03

Facilities and organisation

Both countries have hotels geared to teams. Compare pitch quality, time slots, floodlights, changing rooms and distances rather than room price alone.

04

Cost structure

Total prices depend on dates, hotel category, catering, transfers and extras. Always compare the same service package and watch for extras such as pitch use or referees.

05

Test matches and opponents

Both regions host many teams in season. Request matches early and note preferred level and format.

06

Decision support

Set three to five criteria, weight them, and request a comparable offer for each option. That makes the decision transparent.

More questions and answers

Is Turkey cheaper than Spain?

There is no blanket answer. It depends on dates, hotel, scope and group size. Compare identical packages.

Which destination is better for youth teams?

Both work. Supervision, short distances, catering and a realistic weekly plan matter most.

Can we request both options?

Yes. We are happy to prepare an Antalya offer so you can compare it with other options.

Can test matches be organised?

We request suitable opponents for your dates. Confirmation depends on availability and level.

Where is Antalya and how do teams travel there?

Antalya is on Turkey’s Mediterranean coast. Antalya airport is served from many German-speaking cities; flight time from Germany is usually around three and a half hours.

Is the enquiry binding or chargeable?

The first enquiry is non-binding. Binding services, prices and terms only appear in the written offer.

How large can a group be?

It depends on the hotel and period. State player and staff numbers in your enquiry and we will check capacity.
