Football training camp in Belek – plan it compactly

Belek is one of the best-known resort areas on the Turkish Riviera. For football clubs the key point is that hotels, training facilities and catering sit close together.

Request an offer

Belek is one of the best-known resort areas on the Turkish Riviera. For football clubs the key point is that hotels, training facilities and catering sit close together.

At a glance

  • Transfer from Antalya airport: about 35–45 minutes
  • Hotel choice: pitch proximity, floodlights, meal times
  • Coordinate test matches early
  • Have all details confirmed in writing before booking
01

Location and arrival

Belek lies east of Antalya. The transfer from the airport usually takes about 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic. After the flight there is time for check-in, dinner and a light first session. Still plan a buffer and align it with your arrival time.

02

Why clubs choose Belek

Many properties are used to sports groups: fixed meal times, rooms for team meetings, pool and gym. What matters is not the place name but what the specific hotel can deliver for your squad.

03

Match hotel and pitch

Before booking, clarify whether the pitch belongs to the hotel or is external, which time slots are available, whether floodlights exist and how many teams train at once. Get binding details confirmed in writing.

04

Test matches nearby

Many teams train around Antalya in season. That creates opponents but needs early coordination: age group, level, match format, date and kick-off should be settled before travel.

05

Who Belek suits

Belek suits teams that want a focused weekly plan with short distances: youth teams, amateur clubs, senior teams and academies. If you want variety, Side or Kemer can be added for excursions.

More questions and answers

How far is Belek from Antalya airport?

By transfer it is usually around 35 to 45 minutes, depending on traffic and hotel location.

Are there hotels with their own pitches in Belek?

Yes, some properties offer training pitches. Availability, surface and time slots depend on the hotel and are confirmed in the offer.

When is the best time to travel to Belek?

Spring, autumn and the winter break are popular. See our climate guide for details.

Can test matches be arranged?

We request suitable opponents for your dates. Confirmation depends on availability and level.

Is the enquiry binding or chargeable?

The first enquiry is non-binding. Binding services, prices and terms only appear in the written offer.

How large can a group be?

It depends on the hotel and period. State player and staff numbers in your enquiry and we will check capacity.

When is the best time for a training camp in Antalya?

Winter break, spring and autumn are popular. High summer is very hot, so sessions move to cooler hours.

Which teams do you support?

Youth teams, amateur clubs, senior teams, academies and professional squads.
