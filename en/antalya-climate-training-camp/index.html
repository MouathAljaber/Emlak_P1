Antalya climate – the best time for a training camp

Weather shapes workload, match planning and recovery. The table shows approximate daily highs; the sections explain what that means for your weekly plan.

At a glance

  • Winter: mild, ideal for the winter break
  • Spring: pleasant for preparation
  • Summer: schedule early or late sessions
  • Autumn: warm, less heat
01

Winter and winter break

From December to February it is much milder than in Central Europe, which allows outdoor training during the winter break. Still allow for rain and cooler evenings.

02

Spring: March to May

Temperatures rise noticeably in spring. For many clubs this is a good phase before the second half of the season or the run-in.

03

Summer: plan for the heat

Very high temperatures are normal in summer. Move sessions to early morning and late evening, schedule drink breaks and align workload with the coaching staff.

04

Autumn: September to November

In autumn the sea and air are often still warm while the heat eases. It suits camps at season start or during international breaks.

05

Recovery and the sea

Pool, sea and wellness can support recovery. Availability depends on the hotel and your programme.

More questions and answers

When is the best time to travel?

It depends on your season plan. Winter break, spring and autumn suit many clubs particularly well.

Is it warm enough to train in winter?

Daytime values are usually much milder than in Central Europe. Evenings can be cooler, so pack accordingly.

How do you plan training in summer?

With sessions in the early morning or late evening, plenty of fluids and adjusted workload.

When is the best time for a training camp in Antalya?

Winter break, spring and autumn are popular. High summer is very hot, so sessions move to cooler hours.

Which teams do you support?

Youth teams, amateur clubs, senior teams, academies and professional squads.

Which services are possible?

Hotel, catering, pitches, recovery, test matches, airport transfer and local transport – combined to your wishes.

How does an enquiry with Antalya Camp work?

You send the form with key details; we usually reply within 24 hours with a first assessment and an offer.
