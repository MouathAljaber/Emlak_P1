Nutrition at a training camp – plan your catering

Good catering is part of performance preparation. Reliable timing, suitable choice and clear agreement with the hotel are what count.

Request an offer

Good catering is part of performance preparation. Reliable timing, suitable choice and clear agreement with the hotel are what count.

At a glance

  • Align meal times with the plan
  • Report allergies early
  • Schedule drink breaks
  • Check board type in the offer
01

Fit meal times to the training plan

Set breakfast, lunch and dinner around your sessions. After matches and intense sessions a meal or snack should be available quickly.

02

Allergies and dietary needs

Report allergies, intolerances, vegetarian or halal requirements early. The hotel confirms what is possible.

03

Hydration

Especially in warm weather, drink breaks and drinks at the pitch matter. Plan this with your staff.

04

Board types

Typical options are breakfast only, half board or full board. The right choice depends on programme, budget and hotel.

05

Recovery

A balanced choice with carbohydrates, protein, fruit and vegetables supports recovery. Individual nutrition advice remains with your staff.

More questions and answers

Can special requests be accommodated?

The hotel confirms what is possible. Report requirements as early as you can.

What board is typical?

Often half or full board, depending on hotel and programme.

Who sets the nutrition plan?

The club and its staff. We coordinate the organisation with the hotel.

Which teams do you support?

Youth teams, amateur clubs, senior teams, academies and professional squads.

Which services are possible?

Hotel, catering, pitches, recovery, test matches, airport transfer and local transport – combined to your wishes.

How does an enquiry with Antalya Camp work?

You send the form with key details; we usually reply within 24 hours with a first assessment and an offer.

Can test matches be organised?

We request suitable opponents for your dates. Confirmation depends on availability and level.
