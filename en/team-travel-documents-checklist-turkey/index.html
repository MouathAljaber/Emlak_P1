Travel documents and organisation for clubs

Clean preparation avoids stress at the airport and hotel. This overview helps you organise – always check binding entry rules with the official authorities.

Request an offer

Clean preparation avoids stress at the airport and hotel. This overview helps you organise – always check binding entry rules with the official authorities.

At a glance

  • Check documents early
  • Prepare lists
  • Clarify insurance
  • Keep a packing list
01

IDs and entry

Check in good time which documents everyone needs, including minors and people of other nationalities. Binding rules come from the competent official authorities.

02

Room and squad lists

Prepare lists with names, roles, room wishes and special requirements. It speeds up check-in.

03

Insurance and consent

Clarify travel and accident insurance and, for youth teams, parental consent forms. Ask your association or insurer.

04

Equipment and luggage

Keep training material, balls, medical bag and kits on a packing list and check the airline luggage rules.

05

On-site contact

Name one responsible person and a deputy and share contact details with the hotel and transfer partner.

More questions and answers

Does everyone need a passport?

It depends on nationality and current rules. Check with the official authorities.

What belongs on the packing list?

Kits, balls, medical bag, training wear, documents and chargers – plus your own team specifics.

Who is the contact on site?

Appoint one person and a deputy internally and share their contacts with the hotel and transfer.

Can test matches be organised?

We request suitable opponents for your dates. Confirmation depends on availability and level.

Where is Antalya and how do teams travel there?

Antalya is on Turkey’s Mediterranean coast. Antalya airport is served from many German-speaking cities; flight time from Germany is usually around three and a half hours.

Is the enquiry binding or chargeable?

The first enquiry is non-binding. Binding services, prices and terms only appear in the written offer.

How large can a group be?

It depends on the hotel and period. State player and staff numbers in your enquiry and we will check capacity.
