Hotels with football pitches in Antalya

Choose team-friendly accommodation with practical access to training pitches, meals, recovery and local transport.

Request an offer

A good training camp is not defined by one facility alone. Accommodation, pitch quality, catering and transfer times need to fit a reliable daily schedule.

Check pitch conditions

Specify surface type, intended use, lighting, changing rooms and workload. Confirm final details in writing before booking.

Combine hotel and pitch location

Short distances save team time and reduce transfer risk. Also consider meal times, rest periods and space for team meetings.

Plan transfers as a daily schedule

Airport transport, local transfers and journeys to test matches should match training units, meals and group activities.

Confirm catering arrangements

Share dietary requirements, preferred meal times, allergies and squad size. Individual options remain subject to hotel confirmation.

Schedule recovery

Gym access, pool use or physiotherapy can be added where needed. Availability depends on the selected accommodation and package.

Compare offers consistently

Look beyond the headline price and check included services, cancellation terms, arrival and departure times and likely extras.

Frequently asked planning questions

Are specific hotels or pitches guaranteed?

Availability and names should only be treated as confirmed after the dates and final package are agreed in writing.

What belongs in a daily plan?

Include travel times, meals, pitch slots, recovery and match arrangements so the team has a predictable routine.

Which extras may affect the final price?

Potential extras include local transport, equipment rental, additional pitch use, referees, match fees and changes to the programme.

Prepare the club details for a concrete offer

Share your team type, dates, participant numbers, required services and special requirements.
