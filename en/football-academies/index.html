Football academy camps in Antalya

Give development teams a structured football week with the right training environment, accommodation and practical support.

Request an offer

A successful football camp starts with clear goals and a dependable plan. A complete enquiry makes it easier to compare dates, costs, services and the daily schedule.

01

Set the objectives for the week

Decide whether fitness, tactical preparation, match play or team cohesion is the priority. The answer determines workload and daily structure.

02

Collect travel and participant data

Dates, player and staff numbers, age group, playing level and room requirements provide the basis for an individual offer.

03

Prioritise required services

Choose between accommodation, catering, pitches, transfers, test matches and extras, and distinguish essential services from optional wishes.

04

Check dates and flexibility

School holidays, league schedules and popular training periods affect availability. A second date range can increase the choice of hotels and pitches.

05

Review offer terms

Check included services, payment stages, cancellation terms, room allocation and any likely additional costs.

06

Document internal decisions

A shared checklist for the club, coaches and accompanying staff reduces misunderstandings and helps the trip run smoothly.

Frequently asked planning questions

What information belongs in the first enquiry?

Please include team type, player and staff numbers, level or age group, dates, required services, budget and special requirements.

Is the enquiry binding?

A first enquiry is generally non-binding. Confirmed prices, services and cancellation terms should be stated in the written offer.

How should we prepare departure?

Coordinate travel documents, training equipment, room lists, meal requirements and responsible staff well before departure.

How does an enquiry with Antalya Camp work?

You send the form with key details; we usually reply within 24 hours with a first assessment and an offer.

Can test matches be organised?

We request suitable opponents for your dates. Confirmation depends on availability and level.

Where is Antalya and how do teams travel there?

Antalya is on Turkey’s Mediterranean coast. Antalya airport is served from many German-speaking cities; flight time from Germany is usually around three and a half hours.

Is the enquiry binding or chargeable?

The first enquiry is non-binding. Binding services, prices and terms only appear in the written offer.
